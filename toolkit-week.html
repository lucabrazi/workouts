Notes

  • Zone 2 means an easy pace where you can talk in full sentences, at 80–90 rpm on the bike.
  • If you're sore going into a Toolkit session, drop strength to 2 rounds.
  • If your calves or Achilles feel tight, choose the bike over jump rope.
